Conduit Bend Pro privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

Conduit Bend Pro does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. The app has no account, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising and no network features. It works fully offline.

What stays on your phone

The measurements you type, your selected bender, your edited or custom bender profiles and your settings are saved on your phone so they are there next time you open the app. They never leave your phone. Deleting the app removes them.

Sharing

If you use the share button to send a mark sheet as text or an image, the app hands that text or image to the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes is up to you. The app does not see or keep it.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.
